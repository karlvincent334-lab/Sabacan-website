Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful details before your session.

Is my requested date confirmed when I submit the form?

No. The studio needs to check availability and contact you to confirm your schedule.

What should I bring or wear?

Choose outfits that feel comfortable and suit the look you want. Share any special requests in your booking notes.

Can I reschedule my session?

Please contact the studio as early as possible. Rescheduling depends on availability.

When will I receive my photos?

Delivery timing depends on the session. Confirm the expected turnaround with the studio when your booking is reviewed.

How do I ask about payment or packages?

Include your question in the inquiry form or booking notes so the studio can follow up with the details.

  5. The studio checks availability and contacts you to confirm the schedule, package details and payment instructions.
